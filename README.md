**Effective Date: October 23, 2026**

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the Midnight Vibe application.

We may collect the following categories of information.

- Google Sign-In account identifier
- Name

- Profile information provided through Google
- User ID created for your Midnight Vibe account

### Profile Information
- Age
- Gender
- Languages
- Voice nickname
- User preferences
- Gender filter preferences
- Mood selection
- Other profile settings provided by the user

### App Usage Information

We may collect information about how you use Midnight Vibe, including:

- Matchmaking activity
- Call history metadata
- Connected user information
- Call date and time
- Call duration
- Call status
- Coins balance
- Premium membership status
- Subscription package
- Premium expiry date
- User reports
- Blocked users
- Trust score

- Other interaction and usage information required to operate the app

### Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information such as:

- Device type
- Android operating system version
- App version
- IP address
- Application logs
- Network information
- Push notification token
- Advertising identifiers, where applicable
- Crash and diagnostic information

### Payment and Subscription Information

For purchases and subscriptions, we may process information such as:

- Product ID
- Transaction ID
- Purchase token
- Subscription status
- Subscription verification result
- Purchase or subscription expiry information

Payments are processed through **Google Play Billing**.

We do not directly collect or store your full credit card, debit card, or other payment card details.

### Voice Calling

Midnight Vibe allows users to communicate through live voice calls using third-party voice communication technology.

We do **not intentionally record or store the contents of your live voice conversations**.

However, call-related metadata may be stored for purposes including:

- Call history
- Reconnect functionality
- Safety
- Moderation
- Abuse prevention
- Technical troubleshooting
- Application functionality

- Using safety signals to identify potentially abusive activity

We do not tolerate behavior including:

- Harassment
- Bullying
- Threats
- Abusive behavior

- Sexual exploitation
- Illegal activity

- Spam
- Objectionable content

Users who violate our policies may have their access restricted, suspended, or permanently terminated.
